# Demo: Module 14 — Sprint 3 Wrap-up & Assessment Prep

**Duration:** 8 minutes
**Prerequisite:** none, this is a recap. No new tooling or commands.

## Part 1: What Friday's assessment actually checks (2 min)

Narration: two things, a code submission (your team's schema and queries) and a peer review of
another team's solution. Both are things you've already produced this week, Friday isn't asking
for anything new, it's checking that what you built is genuinely in a clean, reviewable state,
and that you can review someone else's work with the same rigour Module 04 (Sprint 2) taught
for code.

## Part 2: Model a peer-check (4 min)

Pick a volunteer pair. Run through a few checklist items live:

```text
"Show me \d on your model_portfolio_holdings table, walk me through every constraint."
"Run your query proving a CHECK constraint rejects bad data, show me the actual error."
"Explain client_trades to me without looking at the schema."
"Show me your ER diagram, does it still match what's actually in the database?"
```

Narration: same spirit as Sprint 1 and Sprint 2's equivalent modules, a peer-check isn't a test,
it's a second pair of eyes while there's still time to fix a gap, or to genuinely understand a
part of the model you didn't personally build.

## Part 3: Naming this sprint's throughline (2 min)

Narration: Sprint 1 built the tooling foundations, Sprint 2 built the engineering practices,
Sprint 3 built the data systems and modelling skills, from raw SQL, through normalization, ER
diagrams, and constraints, to a real, running, team-owned database that every member can
explain. That's what Friday actually assesses, whether the whole chain holds together, not any
single isolated fact.

## Key message

This module has no new content on purpose. It exists to catch gaps while there's still time to
close them, and to make Friday's assessment a formality rather than a surprise.
