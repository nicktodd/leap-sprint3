# Systems to Classify

For each system below, decide: is it primarily an **OLTP** system, an **OLAP** system, or does
it genuinely have elements of both? Justify your answer with at least one specific reason drawn
from how the system is actually used.

1. **A trading ledger** that records every buy/sell order as it happens, used by the trading
   desk to confirm an order went through correctly.
2. **An executive analytics dashboard** showing quarterly trading volume by asset class, region,
   and advisor, refreshed nightly.
3. **A product catalogue** for an e-commerce site, where customers browse items and add them to
   a basket.
4. **A client onboarding system** that captures a new client's details, identity documents, and
   risk profile as they sign up.
5. **A fraud detection system** that scans millions of historical transactions overnight looking
   for unusual patterns.
6. **An ATM network's core banking system**, processing a withdrawal the instant a customer taps
   their card.
7. **A regulatory reporting system** that compiles a full quarter's transaction history into a
   single submission for a regulator.
8. **A customer support ticketing system**, where an agent looks up and updates a single
   customer's ticket in real time.
